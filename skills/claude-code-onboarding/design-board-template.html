7 design rules to keep in mind
  • One main job per page. One main button. Everything else is quieter.
  • Make the important thing biggest. Size and space show what matters first.
  • Use space. Empty space is not wasted. Crowded pages feel cheap.
  • 2 fonts at most. One for headings, one for text. Body text 16 to 20 px.
  • Colour: 1 brand, 1 accent, calm neutrals. Mostly neutral, a little brand, a touch of accent.
  • Make text easy to read. Strong contrast (the checks below), short lines, big tap targets.
  • Phone first. Most visitors use a phone. Check the Phone view.
